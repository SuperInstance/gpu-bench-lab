======================================================================
SUMMARY: Ternary vs FP32 on RTX 4050 (Ada Lovelace)
======================================================================

  Key findings:
  1. Memory: Ternary achieves ~16× packing density (2-bit vs 32-bit)
  2. Sparsity: ~33% of ternary weights are zero (free skip)
  3. Effective compute: ~67% of operations needed vs dense FP32
  4. Combined advantage: packing × sparsity = significant bandwidth savings

  The bottleneck on edge devices isn't compute — it's memory bandwidth.
  Ternary wins by moving 16× less data through the memory hierarchy.
